## Reading quantities and status

- **Unconfirmed** means no quantity can be established from the source. It is `null` in JSON, not zero. Numeric entries reproduce source values; they do not imply a completed assembly-instance count.
- “pcs/sets” are organizational counting units. Where the source lacks a supplier package unit, the quantity note says so. The 100 m Ethernet-tether entry is a recorded length, not 100 cables.
- Do not add **historical alternatives** to the current quantities. **Optional design files** do not mean that every vehicle includes the mechanism. A CAD part file alone does not establish how many instances are required.
- Resolve **conflicting sources** against the actual vehicle: V4 servos (2/4), relay ratings (30 A/120 A) and different terminal layouts; Pro servo supply (5 V/8.4 V) and terminal quantities.
- Purchase links retain only product IDs from the source and omit account/tracking parameters. Current prices, availability and product options have not been checked. One listing may include ESC/no-ESC, voltage or range options; do not order solely from the URL.
- V4 and V4-pro have distinct thruster wiring, controllers, onboard computers, control signals and CAD. Matching names or listing URLs do not establish interchangeability.

All 26 entries in the original V4 parts table are included. Additional entries from the older electrical document have explicit status, and H30 appears only once as a firmware dependency. Pro connector customization is described alongside devices without separate purchase counts, so device counts have not been copied into connector quantities. For mechanical parts without stated material, tolerance, sealing grade or pressure performance, use the drawings and confirmed vehicle records.
